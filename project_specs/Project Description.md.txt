# Qademy: Multi-Tenant School Management Platform (Zambia)

## 1. Executive Summary
**Qademy** is a localized, cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) School Management System tailored for the Zambian education sector. Designed as a **multi-tenant platform**, Qademy allows multiple independent schools (Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary) to register, configure their unique profiles, and manage their daily operations securely. 

The system seamlessly integrates with Zambian standards, including the **Examinations Council of Zambia (ECZ)** grading formats, local currency (**ZMW**), and widespread mobile money integrations (**MTN MoMo & Airtel Money**).

---

## 2. Multi-Tenant Architecture (SaaS Model)
Because multiple schools use Qademy, data privacy and isolation are paramount. 
* **Tenant Isolation:** Every time a user logs in, the `Auth Service` issues a JWT containing a `TenantID` (School ID). All database queries automatically filter by this `TenantID` to ensure School A cannot see School B's data.
* **Customization:** Schools can upload their own logos, set their own fee structures, and configure custom term dates.
* **Cloud Infrastructure:** Hosted on scalable cloud infrastructure (e.g., AWS or Azure) utilizing microservices to handle high traffic during critical periods, such as end-of-term report generation and ECZ mock exam seasons.

---

## 3. UI/UX Design & Styling Guidelines
Qademy focuses on a **Mobile-First** design, recognizing that many teachers, students, and parents in Zambia will access the platform via smartphones.

### Color Palette & Typography
* **Primary Colors:** **Deep Navy Blue** (`#0B192C`) representing trust and professionalism, and **Vibrant Orange/Gold** (`#FF8C00`) representing African warmth, energy, and youth.
* **Backgrounds:** Clean, off-white/light gray (`#F4F7FE`) to ensure data cards and text remain highly readable.
* **Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif fonts like *Inter* or *Poppins* for excellent readability on small screens.

### Layout Structure
* **Left Navigation:** A sleek dark-blue sidebar (collapsible on mobile) featuring the school's logo alongside the glowing Qademy branding.
* **Top Header:** Global search, notification bell (e.g., for SMS/MoMo alerts), and user profile.
* **Main Dashboard:** Clean white cards with soft drop shadows displaying relevant metrics based on the user's role (e.g., ZMW fee collections for Bursars, pending lesson plans for Management).
